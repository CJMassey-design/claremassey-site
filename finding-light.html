Finding Light

Photographs for a novel

A novel in progress: four generations of women, each with her own camera and her own way of seeing, told in forty-two chapters and forty-two photographs. It grew out of a lost Kodachrome slide and six years of research into how ordinary families photograph themselves, from Kodachrome to Instagram.
