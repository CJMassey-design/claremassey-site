The Key

How each photograph in Finding Light was made.

Every image here was photographed on the same copy stand, under the same lights. Prints are on Fotospeed Platinum Baryta or Permajet Baryta Rag, and were rephotographed. Objects were photographed as they are. The screen images — all of Lila's, and three of Sophie's — are photographs of a phone screen, taken the same way, on the same stand.

The generated images were made with Google's Gemini image model, run inside Adobe Firefly, then composited, retouched, graded, printed and rephotographed like everything else.

So the line below each chapter says only where the picture came from, not what happened to it afterwards. What happened to it afterwards was the same for all of them.

Six origins: photograph — mine · photograph — family · object · object made for the fiction · generated · generated from reference.

Chapter Thirty-Seven

There is no photograph, and that is the photograph.

Darla's war chapters carry no image. This is the one that falls within these forty-two. It is set among real people whose suffering has nothing to do with mine, and I did not want to make a picture of it.

A note on the generated images

The generated images are a closed set. Nothing further will be generated. Where a picture was made rather than found, this key says so, and the number is the number.
