Shared Land

2012, found Kodachrome slides

Using found Kodachrome slides, the work observes our use of the photograph as a mnemonic object, and the relationship between picture and fiction. The appropriated landscapes are of no tangible time or place; they are images of our recent past with a potential future.

Installation, 2012
