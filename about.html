About

I’m Clare Massey, an educator and photographer based in the UK. I spent the 1990s living in New York, and the city never quite let me go.

My work looks at memory, the found photograph and the archive: how we use pictures to hold on to the past, and the fictions we build around them. Shared Land reworks found Kodachrome slides; Industry collects commissioned portraits for clients including BBC Maestro. Finding Light is the result of a six-year project into amateur photography, nostalgia, and the photographs people keep without quite knowing why.

I teach photography theory and practice, and run industry workshops.
